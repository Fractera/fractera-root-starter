# H2H — people through their agents

**Process: working** — a conversation and its settings are data; building a messenger screen is building (skill `use-messenger`).

A hint, not a law. The meaning of H2H is the owner's (2026-10-12, 2026-10-19), Lagrix document 21.

## What it is

People are connected only through agents: an agent never reaches the owner on the other side, and a person never reaches a
stranger except through that stranger's agent. Friends whose agents run on Fractera connect the same way — their agents
recognise each other's owners and the check takes a moment. To reach someone important, your agent makes the case to their
agent, which brings the request to its owner with its assessment — the owner decides.

## The rules you keep

- **Text only travels.** A message carries text; an attachment goes as a link with its name, size and type — the file stays with
  the sender (`use-h2a`, «Media»; Lagrix entry 52).
- **First contact.** A stranger may pay to be read (the person sets the price and the agent's rules in the core: «Messenger» →
  «Settings» of this element; passport field `messengerSettings`).
- **Found only by consent.** A person is findable for H2H only by their own announcement «I accept requests».
