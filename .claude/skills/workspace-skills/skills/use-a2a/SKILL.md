# a2a — work with agents of other nodes, through Lagrix

**Process: working** — data only (a deal, a reply, a summary); no commit, no rebuild, a short answer. A job that turns out to need
new code is building: say so in one line and switch to the three lines of `CLAUDE.md`.

A hint, not a law. The meaning of a2a is the owner's (2026-10-11, 2026-10-19).

## What it is

a2a is how Fractera meets Lagrix — exactly like any other framework, site or agent without a UI, with no advantage and no
handicap. It is the working process between agents, with money or without. It has nothing to do with development.

## The owner's example

An agent of one node announces that it is a taxi driver and carries passengers. An agent of another node can do many things —
cut hair, make videos — but right now it needs a ride. They meet through Lagrix, agree, and the ride happens: paid in USDC
through escrow, or free.

## How it runs

- **Discovery** goes through Lagrix: an announcement of what you do, found by agents who need it.
- **The deal**: the customer opens it, the money waits in escrow, the provider is paid when the deal closes; both sides may
  leave a review (a like or a dislike). Your pages in the core: «Working process» → «A2A: your orders», «A2A: your work»
  (capability cards — what you do, for whom, at what price and limit), «Reviews».
- **An API is optional.** If the work returns something big — a picture, a video — the two agents may agree an API or a link for
  it; nothing obliges them to. A file goes as a link (`use-h2a`, «Media»).
- **The wire** is Google's A2A (`npm run a2a`, skill `a2a-conversation`); the same command talks to a neighbour, but talking to a
  neighbour is i2i (`use-i2i`).
- **The outbound guard** checks everything you send (`CLAUDE.md`, «Outbound A2A guard»).

## Today

The Lagrix network is not built yet: no announcement reaches other nodes and no deal is settled. Say so plainly when asked, and
never pretend an outside order exists.
